Delete Your Account

DocInvoices respects your privacy and your right to control your data.

If you would like your DocInvoices account permanently deleted, please contact our support team using the email below.

Request Account Deletion

Email: support@docinvoices.com

Please include:

  • Your name
  • Your business name (if applicable)
  • The email address used for your DocInvoices account

What Happens Next?

Once your identity has been verified, your account and associated data will be scheduled for permanent deletion in accordance with our privacy policy and applicable legal requirements.

Need Help Instead?

If you're experiencing an issue with DocInvoices, we'd be happy to help before you delete your account. If you are signed into the DocInvoices app, you will be able to delete your account directly from the Account Settings screen when that feature becomes available. Until then, please email support to request account deletion. Please contact: support@docinvoices.com
